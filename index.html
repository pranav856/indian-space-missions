● What is ISRO?

India's Gateway
to the Cosmos

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) is India's national space agency, established in 1969. Renowned for its cost-effective missions and rapid advancements in satellite and launch vehicle technology, ISRO has placed India among the top spacefaring nations on Earth — reaching Mars, the Moon, and the Sun with missions that redefine what's possible on a modest budget.

  • 2023 Chandrayaan-3 lands near lunar south pole — a world first. India becomes 4th nation on the Moon.
  • 2023 Aditya-L1 launched — India's first Sun observatory stationed at the L1 Lagrange point.
  • 2022 ISRO launches a record number of satellites in a single PSLV mission.
  • 2019 Chandrayaan-2 orbiter continues to transmit valuable lunar data from orbit.
