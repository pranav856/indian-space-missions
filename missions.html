All Missions

India's complete space mission archive — from the first satellite to interplanetary exploration.

● Lunar Missions

Lunar · 2008

Chandrayaan-1

India's first lunar probe. Discovered water ice molecules on the Moon's surface using NASA's Moon Mineralogy Mapper.

↗
Lunar · 2019

Chandrayaan-2

Orbiter continues to send valuable lunar data. Lander attempted the first-ever south pole landing — the orbiter remains fully operational.

↗
Lunar · 2023

Chandrayaan-3

First successful lunar south pole landing — a world first that made India the 4th nation to land on the Moon.

↗
Lunar · Planned

LUPEX

Lunar Polar Exploration mission — a joint ISRO-JAXA rover mission to study water ice at the Moon's south pole in the 2020s.

↗

● Planetary Missions

Mars · 2013

Mangalyaan (MOM)

First Asian Mars mission — achieved on the very first attempt at a fraction of Hollywood's budget. Operated for over 7 years.

↗
Venus · Planned 2028

Shukrayaan-1

India's first Venus orbiter — will study Venus's thick atmosphere, volcanic activity, and surface using a synthetic aperture radar.

↗

● Solar Missions

Solar · 2023

Aditya-L1

India's first solar observatory parked at the L1 Lagrange point, 1.5 million km from Earth, studying the Sun continuously.

↗

● Crewed Missions

Crewed · Upcoming

Gaganyaan

India's first crewed spaceflight — a 3-member crew to Low Earth Orbit for 3 days. Will make India the 4th nation to launch humans independently.

↗
Station · Planned 2035

Indian Space Station

India's planned orbital space station — establishing a permanent human presence in space by 2035, building on Gaganyaan technology.

↗

● Astronomy & Science

Astronomy · 2015

AstroSat

India's first dedicated multi-wavelength space observatory — studying black holes and neutron stars in UV, optical, and X-ray wavelengths simultaneously.

↗
Technology · 2024

SPADEX

Space Docking Experiment — made India the 4th country to master orbital docking, a critical technology for future crewed and space station missions.

↗
Technology · 2016

RLV-TD

Reusable Launch Vehicle Technology Demonstrator — India's Space Shuttle prototype that demonstrated hypersonic flight and autonomous landing.

↗

● Launch Vehicles & Records

Record · 2017

PSLV-C37

World record — 104 satellites launched in a single mission on February 15, 2017, surpassing Russia's previous record of 37.

↗
Launch Vehicle · 2017

GSLV Mk III

India's most powerful rocket — capable of carrying 4 tonnes to GTO and 10 tonnes to LEO. Will carry the Gaganyaan crew to orbit.

↗

● Satellites

Communication · 2018

GSAT-11

India's heaviest satellite at 5,854 kg — provides over 16 Gbps broadband connectivity across India, boosting rural internet access significantly.

↗
Navigation · 2018

NavIC

India's own GPS — Navigation with Indian Constellation. 7 satellites providing accurate positioning over India and 1,500 km beyond its borders.

↗
Earth Observation · 2007–2019

Cartosat Series

A series of high-resolution Earth imaging satellites used for cartography, urban planning, disaster management, and border surveillance.

↗
Earth Observation · 2012–2022

RISAT Series

Radar Imaging Satellite series capable of imaging Earth through clouds and at night — critical for disaster monitoring, agriculture, and defence.

↗
Earth Observation · 2003–2016

Resourcesat Series

Multi-spectral Earth observation satellites monitoring land use, crop health, water bodies, and natural resources across India and globally.

↗
Communication · 1983–Present

INSAT Series

Indian National Satellite System — one of the largest domestic communication satellite systems in Asia, supporting TV broadcasting, weather forecasting, and disaster warnings.

↗

● Pioneer Missions

Pioneer · 1975

Aryabhata

India's very first satellite — launched April 19, 1975 on a Soviet rocket. Named after the ancient Indian mathematician. It began India's journey into space.

↗
Pioneer · 1980

SLV-3

India's first indigenous rocket — developed under Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam. Successfully placed the Rohini satellite into orbit in 1980, making India the 7th spacefaring nation.
